## Note on statute references

Title VI of the Civil Rights Act is rendered as 6-بۆلۈم, while Section 117 of the
Higher Education Act is rendered as 117-ماددا. The distinction is deliberate:
بۆلۈم marks the larger subdivision of a statute, ماددا the individual section.

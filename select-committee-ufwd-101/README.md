## About this document

A bipartisan background memorandum explaining the Chinese Communist Party's
united front system: what united front work is, how it is organized under the
Central United Front Work Department, its role in the Sinicization campaign and
the genocide of the Uyghurs in Xinjiang, its development under Xi Jinping, and
the twelve categories of people the system targets.

The memo is of direct relevance to Uyghur readers: it is one of the few
U.S. Government documents that names the UFWD's specific role in surveilling
the Uyghur diaspora, building intelligence databases on it, and mobilizing
community organizations abroad to deflect criticism of Xinjiang policy.

## Notes on the translation

**«بىرلىك سەپ» is a preserved calque.** The Uyghur rendering of *united front*
follows the established PRC-Uyghur term (统一战线 → بىرلىك سەپ) rather than
a fresh translation from English. This is deliberate: the term is the standard
one in Uyghur-language official discourse and is what a reader will encounter
in PRC sources.

**«خىتاي كوممۇنىستىك پارتىيەسى» and «خىتاي كومپارتىيەسى» can be used interchangeably.** The
full form is used at first mention and in formal contexts; the contracted form
is used thereafter. Both refer to the same body.

**Editorial glosses on «شىنجاڭ».** The Uyghur text renders political, colonial and Genocidal term *Xinjiang* as
شىنجاڭ (شەرقىي تۈركىستان), adding the real Uyghur name in parentheses. This is a
translator's addition not present in the English source, retained because it
reflects non-political normal usage among Uyghur readers, just like Tibetens use Tibet, instead of "Xizang."

## About this document

An NCSC counterintelligence bulletin aimed at U.S. state and local officials
and business leaders, explaining how the PRC seeks to influence decision-making
below the federal level. It identifies the actors involved — the CCP's United
Front Work Department, PRC government ministries, embassies and consulates, and
front organizations such as CPAFFC and NACPU — and catalogues five tactics:
collecting personal information on officials, cultivating officials early in
their careers, exploiting sister-city partnerships, creating economic
dependencies, and shaping policy through the business community.

The document pairs closely with the House Select Committee's *United Front 101*
memo in this repository. Where that memo explains the united front system in
general, this bulletin documents how it operates at the subnational level in
the United States, with named cases from the U.S., Germany, the Czech Republic,
Sweden, the Netherlands, and Australia.

## Notes on the translation

**On «خىتاي خەلق جۇمھۇرىيىتى» throughout.** The English uses the abbreviation *PRC* on 
nearly every line. The Uyghur spells the name out in full with the Latin
abbreviation in parentheses on each occurrence, following the convention used
elsewhere in this repository for organizations identified by acronym in the
source.

**Organization names carry their English acronym.** CPAFFC, NACPU, UFWD, MI5 and
similar bodies are given a full Uyghur rendering at first mention, then referred
to by the original Latin acronym. This keeps the text searchable against the
English source and avoids inventing Uyghur acronyms that have no currency.

**On «سۇبيېكت» for *entity*.** Consistent with the analysis in [`README.md`](../R49036-transnational-repression/README.md) (R49036-transnational-repression), which sets out why سۇبيېكت is preferred over ئورۇن, ئىگىلىك, and گەۋدە for legal and policy uses of *entity*.

**Personal and place names.** Given in Uyghur transcription with the original
Latin spelling in parentheses at first mention — كرىستوفر رېي (Christopher Wray),
مېلبۇرن (Melbourne), كېنتاكى (Kentucky).
